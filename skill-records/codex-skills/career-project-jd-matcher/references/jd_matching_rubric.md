# JD Matching Rubric

## Score Dimensions

Use a 100-point score:

- 35 points: hard requirements supported by resume or project evidence.
- 20 points: core responsibilities resemble completed work.
- 15 points: tool and workflow overlap.
- 10 points: business domain overlap.
- 10 points: seniority and scope fit.
- 10 points: resume-tailoring feasibility without fabrication.

## Recommendation Bands

- 80-100: `strong apply`
- 65-79: `apply with tailored framing`
- 50-64: `maybe after confirmation`
- Below 50: `do not prioritize`

## Red Flags

Downgrade even when keywords match if:

- The JD requires a credential, degree, language, location, or years of experience that the user does not have.
- Most matching terms appear only as inferred tags, not strong evidence.
- The role is much more senior than the user's supported experience.
- The tailored resume would require unsupported metrics or responsibilities to appear competitive.

## Reverse Search Keywords

Derive job search keywords from strong evidence tags. For this user's likely project history, useful families may include:

- Python Excel automation
- Finance data analyst
- Procurement data analyst
- SAP data analyst
- Business process automation
- Reporting automation
- Data operations analyst
- BI reporting analyst
- RPA analyst
- Financial systems analyst

Do not present these as guaranteed fit. Use them as starting points and re-score actual JDs.

## Output Table Fields

When recommending jobs, include:

- `rank`
- `job_title`
- `company`
- `source`
- `score`
- `recommendation`
- `supported_matches`
- `gaps_or_risks`
- `resume_strategy`
- `new_project_evidence_to_add`
- `needs_user_confirmation`
