# Visual Recipe

Use these values as flexible targets, not rigid numeric corrections. Adapt them to the source photograph.

## Default treatment

- Exposure: preserve the original or lower very slightly, typically no more than about one third of a stop.
- White balance: gently warm while keeping whites and skin credible.
- Contrast: restrained; retain separation without hard black and white clipping.
- Highlights: compress softly to preserve skies, windows, pale walls, and skin sheen.
- Shadows: keep readable and atmospheric; do not lift them into a flat HDR look.
- Saturation: modestly restrained, especially greens, oranges, and blues.
- Sharpness and clarity: low to moderate; preserve detail without crunchy edges.
- Grain: fine, subtle, and consistent with image size; never use grain to hide artifacts.
- Vignette: absent or nearly imperceptible.

## Depth hierarchy

Prefer one of these arrangements:

1. Soft nearby foreground, clear middle-ground subject, calm distant context.
2. Soft edge obstruction, clear distant street or landscape, substantial negative space.
3. Clear human gesture or small subject within a wider environmental frame.

Blur only elements that could realistically sit close to the lens. Use soft transitions and preserve recognizable material texture.

## Editing prompt scaffold

```text
Edit the supplied travel photograph non-destructively.

Primary change: <crop/rebalance/foreground blur/tonal treatment>.
Composition: <subject position, negative space, environmental framing>.
Depth: <soft plausible foreground; clear middle or distant subject>.
Light: preserve natural available light; slightly subdued exposure; soft highlight roll-off; readable shadows.
Color: gently warm, restrained saturation, natural skin and material colors.
Texture: preserve pores, fabric, stone, foliage, haze, and fine detail; optional subtle fine film grain.
Keep unchanged: identity, location, architecture, meaningful objects, believable perspective, and documentary character.
Avoid: HDR, halos, commercial preset look, teal-orange grading, plastic skin, excessive denoising, oversharpening, fake portrait-mode edges, warped geometry, duplicated objects, and obvious AI artifacts.
```

## Validation checklist

- Confirm the subject and location still look like the original.
- Check faces, hands, text, railings, foliage, furniture, and architectural lines.
- Check that foreground blur follows believable distance and occlusion.
- Confirm highlights are not clipped and shadows are not crushed or unnaturally lifted.
- Confirm warmth and grain remain subtle at full-image and close-up views.
- Reject outputs that look more polished, staged, or commercial than the source.
